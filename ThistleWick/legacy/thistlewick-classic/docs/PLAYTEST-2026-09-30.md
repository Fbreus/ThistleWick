# Thistlewick playtest — 30 September 2026

The main balance problem is that gathering has little cost or intention for many resources. Natural loose items still collect automatically, and every harvestable plant or scenery pickup disappears in one action. Increasing tree hit counts alone would miss most of the problem: ordinary trees already take 13–16 hand hits, and axes meaningfully improve that.

There are also several bugs worth fixing before tuning difficulty: lost harvests when the pack is full, actions doing something other than expected, and an Elder shield progression blocker.

## Scope and evidence

This was an **automated desktop browser playthrough plus controlled mechanics checks**, using Chrome/Edge through Puppeteer. It was not an uninterrupted, unassisted run to the ending. Keyboard movement and pack/journal controls were exercised in the browser; targeted cases used a separate instrumented build to select targets, supply test inventories, advance time, and reach later systems. The existing smoke checks cover additional sleep, gardening, cottage, resident, save/reload, and boss flows through debug hooks.

- Main review world: seed **-1421759901**, generator **3**. A second fresh world supplied the follow-up checks.
- Opening progression exercised: harvesting wood → planks/sticks → placed workbench → wooden axe/pickaxe → placed campfire → roasted mushroom → crafted bed. Rock and iron tool requirements were checked.
- Screens inspected at 1280×800 and 800×600. Software-rendered headless timing is unsuitable for judging normal gameplay frame rate or completion time.
- Source reviewed to explain the observed behavior and inspect progression beyond the scripted route. Source-only findings are identified below.
- Raw evidence: [observations](playtest-2026-09-30/observations.json), [follow-up checks](playtest-2026-09-30/followup.json).
- Screenshots: [opening](playtest-2026-09-30/02-start.png), [pack](playtest-2026-09-30/03-pack.png), [journal](playtest-2026-09-30/04-journal.png), [small screen](playtest-2026-09-30/05-small-screen.png).

Gameplay source was not changed for this review. The pre-existing working-tree changes were left intact.

## Fix first: reproducible bugs and progression failures

### 1. Elder shield can become permanently unobtainable — critical

**Confirmed in a controlled browser check and source.** The Elder drops 2 or 3 carapace, with equal probability. The shield recipe requires 3. Once the Elder dies, `kill:elder` prevents natural respawning. Forcing the legitimate two-item outcome produced `carapace: 2`, `needed: 3`, and `naturalRespawnAllowed: false`.

**Improve:** Guarantee enough carapace for the shield, and protect it from permanent loss or provide another source. Merely guaranteeing 3 on the first kill is insufficient: death halves resource stacks, so 3 carapace can become 2 before crafting.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — `hitEnemy`, `respawn`; `src/systems/boss.ts` — `shouldSpawn`; `src/items.ts` — Elder shield recipe.

### 2. A full pack destroys harvest rewards — high

**Confirmed in the browser.** Felling a tree with a full pack removed the tree and awarded zero wood. Harvesting a ripe crop removed the crop and awarded zero roots and seeds. `giveItem` reports a full pack but does not preserve the remainder. The same source pattern affects mined nodes, dug blocks, recovered stations, berries, and enemy rewards. Ordinary scenery pickups already refuse collection when there is no room.

**Improve:** Keep uncollected rewards as world drops, or preserve the source until all rewards fit. Handle multi-item yields and partial stacks consistently. Rare boss rewards need the same protection.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — `giveItem`, `fellTree`, `harvestCrop`, `harvestBush`, `hitNode`, `hitBlock`, `digCell`.

### 3. Grass intercepts eating — high

**Confirmed in a controlled browser check.** With berries selected, hunger at 30, and grass targeted, Use harvested grass; hunger stayed 30 and all 3 berries remained. `useItem` processes a targeted pickup before the selected food. This makes eating unreliable in a densely vegetated world.

**Improve:** Give food use an explicit, reliable action. Keep gathering on E or a clearly separate context action. Prompts must state what the next click will actually do.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — `useItem`.

### 4. E silently uproots unripe crops — high

**Confirmed in the browser.** E removed a freshly planted crop and returned its seed. The prompt says “hit to uproot,” suggesting E is safe. A player checking growth can erase it accidentally.

**Improve:** E should inspect an unripe crop and harvest a ripe one. Reserve uprooting for a deliberate hit or hold action.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — `interact`, `harvestCrop`, `updateAimVisuals`.

### 5. Crafting rejects recipes that would free their own output slot — high

**Confirmed in the browser.** With 23 full stone stacks and one slot containing one wood, crafting two planks returned “Your pack is full.” Consuming that wood would have freed a slot for the planks.

**Improve:** Validate capacity against the inventory after ingredient consumption, then commit the whole transaction atomically.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — `craft`, `canHold`.

### 6. Stronger tools do not satisfy the opening journal — high

**Confirmed in the browser.** Crafted a stone pickaxe without crafting a wooden one. The active goal remained “Craft a wooden pickaxe at the workbench.” Stone tools only require loose stone, sticks, fibre, and a workbench, so skipping wood is an attractive route.

**Improve:** Decide whether skipping a tier is intended. If yes, accept an equal-or-better tool in the journal. If no, make substantial stone gathering depend on the wooden pickaxe rather than litter collection.

**Location:** `src/items.ts` — tool recipes; `src/systems/journal.ts` — wooden pickaxe goal.

### 7. Inventory and journal do not pause survival — high

**Confirmed in the browser and source.** With the pack open, 10 simulated seconds reduced hunger by 2.4. Escape with no overlay left the game in `play`. Enemy updates also continue while reading the pack/journal, because these overlays set `uiOpen` without leaving `play`.

**Improve:** Provide a real pause menu and make the single-player overlay behavior explicit. Prefer pausing survival while players learn recipes or read long journal entries. Preserve any intentional unpaused mode as a setting.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — `toggleInv`, `toggleJournal`, keyboard handler, `frame`, `updatePlayer`.

### 8. Berry cooldowns reset when scenery regenerates — medium

**Confirmed in a controlled browser regeneration check.** A harvested bush had `ready: false, regrow: 90`; regenerating its chunk immediately produced `ready: true, regrow: 0`. The save contains no bush regrowth state.

**Improve:** Store a next-ready game timestamp per bush and restore it across chunk unloads and save/reload. Otherwise travel or reload bypasses the food cooldown.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — bush generation, `harvestBush`, `saveGame`.

## Gathering and survival balance

### 9. Walking still gathers natural resources — high

**Confirmed in the browser.** A loose stone collected with no interaction while the player was one block away. The automatic pickup radius is about **1.225 blocks** (`distance² < 1.5`). Loose stone, sticks, fibre, and mushrooms all use this path.

In the main world's 24-block clearing area there were **29 automatic pickups**: 9 stone, 9 sticks, 8 fibre, 3 mushrooms. This is available supply, not a measured collection rate. A 40-second movement-only simulation collected only one stone and one mushroom; terrain/collision stopped parts of that route. The evidence confirms unwanted passive collection, but does not establish that every walk floods the inventory.

**Improve:** Require E for natural loose resources by default. Keep optional automatic collection for loot the player deliberately created by chopping/mining. Distinguish both visually. Reducing the radius alone will not solve the lack of intention.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — pickup generation, `pickups`.

### 10. Every plant and scenery resource takes one action — high

**Confirmed in the browser.** Grass, fern, leaf litter, twig, loose stone, both mushroom types, and wildflower all disappeared after one E interaction. Tool strength is irrelevant to these harvests. Some visually substantial stones and long twigs use the same action cost as tiny pickups.

**Improve:** Give rooted or substantial resources harvest progress, shared by every interaction path. E must not bypass the new hit requirement. Keep truly loose objects and food quick to pick up; their cost can be deliberate selection rather than repeated punching.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — `harvestPickup`, `interact`, `useItem`, `resolveSwing`.

### 11. Almost every grass tuft is an economic resource — high

**Observed density plus source-derived balance concern.** The sampled clearing contained **403 pickups**, including **284 harvestable grass tufts**, 42 leaf-litter pieces and 12 ferns. The 340 fibre-producing plants/litter alone could fund 113 bandages at 3 fibre each; a bandage restores 35 health. Collecting them still takes deliberate actions, but the safe supply is disproportionately large.

**Improve:** Keep most grass decorative. Use recognisable harvestable patches or bundles, reduce free fibre supply, and review bandage economics together. Tune resource availability per area as well as hit counts. Do not reduce visual forest density just to reduce rewards.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — grass/scenery generation; `src/items.ts` — bandage recipe and healing.

### 12. Cooking and farming have weak competition from easy food — medium

**Source-derived balance concern, cooking flow exercised.** A day is 300 seconds; idle hunger consumption is 72 per day. A roasted mushroom restores 34 hunger and costs only one mushroom at a campfire, without fuel or cooking time. Roughly three mushrooms cover a non-sprinting day. Bushes also provide 2–4 berries every 90 seconds, before the regeneration exploit above.

**Improve:** Test whether gardening meaningfully improves reliability or efficiency over foraging. First fix passive pickups and berry persistence; then consider fuel, brief cooking time, biome-dependent food availability, or other worthwhile benefits for grown food. Avoid increasing hunger drain before controls and gathering are fixed.

**Location:** `src/constants.ts`, `src/items.ts`, `src/main.ts` — hunger, cooking, berries.

### 13. The protected clearing weakens the urgency to build shelter — design decision

**Source/design assessment; sanctuary protections exercised by the survival smoke before its later failure.** Permanent beetle protection is valuable for a relaxed mode, but it also removes much of the first night's shelter pressure. Abundant protected fibre and food amplify this.

**Improve:** Preserve the safe clearing for the intended cozy/accessibility experience. If a tougher survival experience is desired, offer an explicit difficulty choice and give exploration resources or objectives that make leaving worthwhile. Judge shelter pacing in that chosen mode rather than silently removing safety.

## Controls, feedback, and usability

### 14. Gathering can unexpectedly change what left click does — high

**Confirmed in a controlled browser check.** Selecting stone changed Use into placement. A click aimed at a tree did not swing and displayed “You cannot place that there.” `addItem` fills the first free slot, so auto-picking stone into an empty selected slot can trigger this switch without an equipment choice.

**Improve:** Provide a reliable empty-hand selection or separate attack and placement actions. Newly acquired items should not silently change the selected action. Show the selected item name and current action alongside the hotbar.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — `addItem`, `useItem`, `toolStats`, hotbar selection.

### 15. A missed E interaction opens the entire pack — medium

**Confirmed in the browser.** E without a valid target opens the inventory. Repeatedly trying to reach a plant can interrupt gathering with an unrelated overlay.

**Improve:** Let an unsuccessful world interaction do nothing or show “Move closer.” Keep Tab as the dedicated pack action.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — final branch of `interact`.

### 16. Long harvests need progress feedback — medium

**Source/UI assessment.** Voxel digging displays a percentage, but tree, fallen-log, ancient-trunk, and rock-node prompts do not show remaining harvest progress. Increasing hand hits without feedback would make these interactions feel unresponsive.

**Improve:** Add an outline for the active target and a short progress bar, clear hit feedback, and a tool recommendation. Use the same feedback for plants if they receive multiple-hit harvesting. Stop a held harvest when its target is gone so it does not unexpectedly affect the object behind it.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — `updateAimVisuals`, `hitNode`, held-use loop.

### 17. The pack needs practical ways to manage a gathering game — medium

**UI/source assessment.** The pack has 24 slots and whole-slot swapping. There are no implemented split-stack, quick-transfer, discard/drop, sorting, or storage-chest actions. This becomes more painful as tools occupy individual slots and gathering supplies several resources.

**Improve:** Start with quick hotbar transfer, stack merging/splitting, a safe drop action, and a storage container. Do this alongside reward-overflow handling.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — inventory/`slotEl`; `src/items.ts` — available items.

### 18. Crafting is a long unfiltered list with one click per batch — medium

**Observed in the pack and confirmed in source.** Unavailable tools and stations appear alongside immediate recipes. Repeated plank production requires repeated Craft clicks. The opening journal says to craft a workbench but the player must infer the intermediate plank conversions.

**Improve:** Add “Craftable now,” category filters, batch quantities, and a small ingredient chain for the tracked goal. Show missing station/material requirements clearly without hiding future progression entirely.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — `renderInv`; `index.html` — crafting panel.

### 19. HUD elements overlap on smaller desktop screens — medium

**Observed at 800×600.** The hotbar overlaps the hunger/stamina panel, and the large tutorial toast intrudes on the goal panel. At 1280×800, the pack/journal close hint occupies the same top-right area as the clock text.

**Improve:** Reserve layout space for status bars and the hotbar, constrain toast placement, and put each overlay's close control inside its panel. Check intermediate widths as well as the existing 760px breakpoint. Use a notification queue so resource messages do not replace important tutorial instructions.

**Evidence:** [small-screen screenshot](playtest-2026-09-30/05-small-screen.png), [pack screenshot](playtest-2026-09-30/03-pack.png).

### 20. Death says items were dropped, but they are deleted — medium

**Confirmed in the browser and source.** The death message says “You dropped half of your gathered resources.” Respawn directly halves stack quantities and creates no recoverable grave/drop. A 3-carapace stack became 2; 12 stone became 6. Tools remained intact.

**Improve:** Either create a recoverable grave/backpack or say “lost” clearly. Protect unique progression resources or make them renewable.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — `die`, `respawn`.

### 21. Cottage failures need an actionable explanation — medium

**Source-derived UX finding; cottage move-in/out flow passed the existing smoke assertions.** The pure cottage checks can report missing enclosure, space, bed, light, or workbench, but the gameplay scan silently skips invalid candidate homes. A resident leaving produces a generic unsafe-home message.

**Improve:** Add an inspect-home action on the bed or doorway that lists unmet requirements and, ideally, highlights enclosure gaps. Give a short grace period during renovations before a resident leaves.

**Location:** `src/main.ts` — `cottageAt`, `checkHomes`; `src/systems/homestead.ts` — `checkCottage`.

## Proposed gathering tuning for the next pass

These are **starting targets to playtest**, not validated final balance. Apply action routing, target feedback, and resource-density fixes first.

| Resource | Current behavior | Suggested first experiment |
|---|---|---|
| Natural loose stone/stick | Automatic within ~1.225 blocks | One deliberate E pickup; no walking collection |
| Truly loose mushroom/berry | One action; some mushrooms auto-collect | Keep one deliberate pick; avoid turning eating into a repetitive chore |
| Harvestable grass patch | One action, one fibre | 2–3 hand hits or equivalent hold time; most grass decorative |
| Fern/rooted fibre plant | One action, one fibre | 3–4 hand hits; appropriate tool reduces it to 1–2 |
| Substantial branch/twig | One action, one stick | 2–3 hand hits; axe 1 hit; tiny loose sticks remain pickups |
| Substantial embedded stone | Some scenery stones use one-action collection | 3–4 hand hits for a small fragment, or require a wooden pickaxe; distinguish from loose pebbles |
| Ordinary tree | 13–16 hand hits; sampled tree: 14 / 6 / 4 / 3 with hands / wooden / stone / iron axe | Initially keep existing health; if still too quick, test 16–20 hand hits while preserving a strong axe advantage |
| Fallen log | 18 hand hits | Test 22–28 hand hits and a clear axe advantage |
| Ancient trunk | 55 hand hits, 24 wood | Prefer a visible axe requirement rather than a much longer hand-punching chore |
| Rock / iron outcrop | Requires wooden / stone pickaxe respectively | Preserve these gates; fix loose-resource bypasses first |

A sampled ordinary tree produced 5 wood at every tool tier. Hit counts were **14 by hand, 6 wooden axe, 4 stone axe, 3 iron axe**. That is already a useful tool progression. Making plants and scenery deliberate, scarce enough, and consistent should come before broadly multiplying tree health.

## Validation and remaining coverage

- `npm.cmd run build`: passed for the ordinary game build.
- `npm.cmd test`: **112 tests passed across 13 files**.
- `npm.cmd run smoke`: gameplay assertions completed, but the command failed on external-resource `ERR_NETWORK_ACCESS_DENIED` console errors in this environment. The page references Google Fonts. Do not report this suite as passing; record request URLs on future failures or bundle fonts locally.
- `npm.cmd run smoke:survival`: failed at **“generated wood source: Fallen log”** because the random test world had no matching nearby log. Earlier assertions reached sanctuary/wilderness behavior, eight harvest categories, full-pack pickup handling, and camera-targeted grass harvesting. Later reload/persistence assertions were not reached in that run. Use fixed seeds or deterministic fixtures for this regression test.
- Separate instrumented browser checks produced the linked JSON and screenshots. The main instrumented run recorded no page/console errors.

Still needs human playtesting on a normal GPU: sustained combat feel, boss telegraph readability, camera comfort under dense canopy, deliberate construction of a full cottage, long cave expeditions, complete narrative quests, touch controls, audio, and progression pacing across multiple seeds. Boss damage/debug checks do not validate combat difficulty. These are follow-up coverage areas, not claims that those systems are broken.

Recommended work order: fix reward/progression loss → resolve action ambiguity → remove passive natural-resource collection → tune harvest effort and density → improve feedback/inventory → run fresh-start human playtests through the first night and then the Elder.
